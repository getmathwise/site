LaTeX math for Confluence Cloud

Write LaTeX with /math and a live preview, or type $...$ in the page. Mathwise renders it with MathJax 4, in light and dark themes, and in PDF and Word export. It runs entirely on Atlassian's platform.

Coming soon to the Atlassian Marketplace. Free for up to 10 users.

LaTeX that renders as written

MathJax 4 with the AMS packages, mathtools, physics, mhchem, braket, cancel and commutative diagrams. Your TeX is never rewritten. Equations are numbered across the page, and \eqref works between formulas. A space admin can define \newcommands once for every formula in the space.

With the text, in dark mode, in export

Inline formulas flow with the text, colours follow Confluence's light and dark themes, and display equations arrive with the page. Formulas come through PDF and Word export.

An editor that helps

A live preview, command suggestions as you type, and a palette of symbols, structures and templates. A formula with an error cannot be saved, so readers never see a broken one.

Switch from another math app

An admin converts the formulas of other Confluence math apps, and Data Center LaTeX macros, into Mathwise formulas in place, space by space. Anything Mathwise cannot convert stays as it was and is listed.

Support

Read the user guide, or ask a question through the support form or by email to mathwise@officehoursmath.com. Mathwise stores no personal data and sends nothing outside Atlassian: see the privacy policy.
